# Okyema brand assets

Production starter pack for the approved O/K monogram.

## Source artwork

- `okyema-mark.svg` — canonical standalone mark
- `okyema-mark-light.svg` — tuned for light surfaces
- `okyema-mark-dark.svg` — tuned for dark surfaces
- `okyema-logo-light.svg` — horizontal logo for light surfaces
- `okyema-logo-dark.svg` — horizontal logo for dark surfaces
- `background-light.svg` and `background-dark.svg` — scalable app backgrounds

## Generated assets

- `app-icons/` — installable PNG icons from 16px to 1024px
- `favicon/` — SVG, PNG and ICO favicons
- `backgrounds/` — PNG app backgrounds at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes

## Colours

- Obsidian `#0B1020`
- Indigo `#151D3B`
- Intelligence Violet `#7457FF`
- Luminous Cyan `#36D7E8`
- Cloud `#F5F7FB`
- White `#FFFFFF`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 20% of its diameter. Do not add tails, dots, orbit lines, shadows or decorative elements to the symbol.
